# Release preview review

Two 29-second editions use actual production SwiftUI views with controlled bibliographic demo data. No invented UI, simulated cursor, book-body contents, voiceover, or music is present. The film is intentionally silent for muted App Store playback; a silent stereo AAC track is included in the store export. Native screenshots are camera-edited, not a recording of mouse interaction.

Both language compositions passed lint, runtime, layout, and contrast checks with zero errors. Reviewed the beat/contact sheets, cut boundaries, and phone-size sheet. Still-reading holds and silence warnings were kept deliberately. Product text is most legible at desktop playback; the full-workspace theme shots are the weakest at phone width. With more time, a live native interaction recording would show editing actions more naturally than these captured states.

Final exports: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 29 seconds, H.264 High Level 4.0, 10 Mbps CBR target, stereo AAC at 48 kHz. Codec, duration, dimensions, and decoded frames are checked with FFmpeg. Audio contains deliberate silence; no listening check is needed to judge a music mix.
